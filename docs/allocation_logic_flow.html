OptiFlow Business Logic

Allocation & Replenishment Pipeline
1. Initialization & Deficit Calc
Determine Net Deficit per Store based on Planogram Target minus Live SOH.
The Matching Cascade
🛡️ Must Match Brand
🛡️ Must Match Commodity
💰 Must Be Within ±20% MRP
❌ Max 3 Colors per Model
❌ Max 2 Units per SKU
❌ Maintain ≥ 85% Brand Uniqueness
Tier 1

Recent Winners

Prioritize exactly what is selling right now.

  • Find exact SKUs sold in the Last X Days (User Selected in UI).
  • If Warehouse has stock, allocate exact SKU.
  • Stop if deficit is filled.
Tier 2

Historical Winners

If Tier 1 is out of stock, fall back to long-term proven sellers.

  • Find exact SKUs sold in the Last 6 Months.
  • If Warehouse has stock, allocate exact SKU.
  • Stop if deficit is filled.
Tier 3

Attribute Substitute

If exact SKUs are entirely out of stock, find the closest substitute.

  • Relax Color (Find same frame, diff color)
  • Relax Material
  • Relax Shape
3. Final Dispatch Order
Lock allocations, apply FIFO (oldest warehouse stock leaves first), and generate Dispatch File.
